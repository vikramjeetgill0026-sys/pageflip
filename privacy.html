Privacy Policy

How PageFlip handles information when you visit the website or contact the team.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. Information you provide

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, payment reference and support message. Payment details are handled by the payment provider; PageFlip does not ask you to send card or UPI PIN details in chat.

2. Payment processing

Payments are handled by the third-party payment gateway displayed at checkout. That provider processes payment information under its own privacy policy and security practices.

3. Website storage

This website uses browser local storage to remember the start time of the launch-offer countdown so it continues across page refreshes on the same browser. The site may also use essential technical logs provided by its hosting service.

4. How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and provide purchased access or support.
  • To verify payment-related support requests.
  • To maintain, protect and improve the website and service.
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations where applicable.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers involved in payment processing, hosting or communications when needed to operate the service, or where required by law.

6. Retention and security

We retain communications and transaction-related information for as long as reasonably needed for support, business records and legal obligations. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and contact

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal record-keeping requirements. Email insaneviralguy@gmail.com.
