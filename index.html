FAQ

No confusing promises. Just the model.

What exactly am I buying for ₹299?

The PageFlip Starter System: the core automated content workflow/training, high-potential content research process, content organization and publishing framework, plus the roadmap for building and eventually listing a page.

Is PageFlip only a Reel downloader?

No. The downloader/collection workflow is the hero tool, but the business model is Build → Grow → Sell: build a page, grow a real audience, then potentially list the page on the PageFlip Marketplace.

What does ≈99% automation mean?

It refers to the goal of systemizing the repetitive parts of the supported workflow. It does not mean 99% of your business is automatic, and you still control niche, sources, content choices, review and publishing decisions.

Can I really publish 80+ Reels a day?

The workflow is designed around an 80+ posts/day high-volume target when properly configured. Actual posting capability depends on your account, platform rules, scheduling tools and other limits. It is not a guarantee of 80+ posts or views every day.

Will 10% of my Reels go viral?

No specific viral percentage is guaranteed. The 10% figure shown on the page is only an illustrative planning example: 80 posts × 10% = 8 potential outperformers. Actual performance can be much higher or lower.

Will I get real followers automatically?

The system is designed to create more opportunities for real audience growth through consistent, high-potential content. Followers are never guaranteed or automatically created by PageFlip.

How do I make money from a grown page?

Build the page, grow its genuine audience and engagement, then list it on the PageFlip Marketplace. The page can be offered to potential buyers; the final sale price and whether a sale occurs depend on market demand and the page's actual value.

Can I use any creator's Reel?

No. Use only content you own, have permission to use, or are otherwise legally entitled to reuse. PageFlip does not bypass access controls or grant copyright permission.
